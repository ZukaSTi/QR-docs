# Wallet Watcher

> Track other wallets without importing their keys.

---

## What It Is

**Wallet Watcher** lets you follow public addresses — famous wallets or any address you care about — and see balances (and, on Premium, more activity) without holding those keys.

This is **watch-only**. You cannot send from a watched wallet.

---

## How to Use

1. Open **Wallet Watcher**
2. Search an address or ENS, or pick a **famous wallet** chip
3. Add it to your watchlist
4. Pull to refresh balances

## FAQ

**Is this the same as importing a wallet?**

No. Importing uses a seed or private key and gives you full control. Watcher only **reads** public data.

**Can I watch Bitcoin or Dash addresses?**

Follow what the in-app search accepts for your build. EVM addresses are the primary path.
