# Identity Verification (KYC)

> A one-time check that unlocks cards and higher payment limits.

---

## When It's Needed

| Feature | Verification |
|---|---|
| [QR Payments](qr-payments.md) | The app asks before your first payment if your country requires it. Verification can also raise your payment limits |
| [Virtual Cards](cards.md) | Always required before issuing a card |
| [Buy Crypto](buy-crypto.md) | Asked by the purchase partner, depending on the amount and country |

Verification is done inside the app: a photo of your ID document and a short selfie check.

---

## Documents

Use a valid, original government-issued ID, such as a passport, national ID card or driver's licence. The app shows which documents are accepted for your country.

**Before you take the photo:**
- The whole document is in the frame, with no cropped corners
- No glare, blur or fingers over the text
- The document is not expired
- The name matches the name you enter in the app

{% hint style="danger" %}
Verify only with **your own** documents. Using another person's document or edited photos leads to rejection and can block the features that require verification. Your wallet and funds stay yours.
{% endhint %}

---

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Not started | You haven't submitted documents | Start verification |
| Under review | Your documents are being checked | Wait. Don't start a second verification |
| Approved | Done | Nothing |
| Rejected | The check did not pass | Read the reason in the app, fix it and submit again |

**Common reasons for rejection:** blurry or cropped photo, glare, an expired document, a name that doesn't match the document, or a selfie that doesn't match the photo.

---

## Stuck in Review?

Most checks finish quickly, but some go to manual review. If your status stays **Under review** for more than 24 hours, create a [support ticket](../welcome/support.md).

---

## Known Issues

- **Ukraine:** only a **driver's licence** or an **international (foreign travel) passport** can be used for now. The internal ID card is temporarily not accepted.
