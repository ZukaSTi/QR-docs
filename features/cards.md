# Virtual Cards

> Spend crypto like a card — coming soon.

---

## Status

**Virtual cards** are in final preparation and are **not in the current app release**. They will go live shortly.

This page describes what the feature is for, so you know what to expect.

---

## What It Will Do

A **virtual card** lets you spend assets from [QR] wallet anywhere cards are accepted online (and, depending on rollout, in-app wallets).

Typical flow once live:

1. Open the **Cards** section
2. Complete identity verification (KYC) — required to issue a card
3. Issue a virtual card
4. Use the card details in online checkout or add them to your phone wallet when that option is enabled

[QR] wallet does not hold your keys. Card issuing still requires identity checks under card-network rules.

---

## What We Will Not Ask You To Do

- You will not send funds to a random chat or support account to "activate" a card
- Card details will appear only inside the official app
