# Virtual Cards

> Spend your crypto anywhere Visa is accepted — online, and in stores with Apple Pay or Google Pay.

---

## Availability

Virtual Visa cards are live in the [QR] wallet app. Update the app to the latest version and open the **Cards** section. Issuing a card requires a one-time [identity verification](identity-verification.md).

---

## Costs and Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Card issue | **$10**, one-time |
| First top-up | Minimum **6 USDT** — activates the card |
| Later top-ups | Minimum **2 USDT**, maximum **1,000 USDT** per top-up |
| Top-up fee | **2%** of the top-up amount |
| Cards per user | Up to 3 |

Top-up minimums can change — the current amounts and the final total are always shown before you confirm.

{% hint style="info" %}
The issue fee is not a deposit — it is not added to your card balance. Your card balance starts with your first top-up.
{% endhint %}

---

## How to Get a Card

1. Open **Cards** and start issuing a card
2. Complete identity verification (once)
3. Pay the issue fee
4. Make your first top-up (at least 6 USDT) — the card becomes active

---

## Topping Up

Top up from your wallet with **USDC or USDT** on Solana, BNB Chain or Ethereum, or **USDC** on Base. Polygon is not supported for top-ups.

A top-up is sent as two blockchain transfers. The card balance updates after both are confirmed.

---

## Using the Card

- **Card details** (number, expiry, CVV) are shown only inside the app.
- **3DS / confirmation code** — when an online shop asks for a code, open the card in the app: the code is shown there.
- **Freeze / unfreeze** the card at any time from the card screen.
- **Transactions** — see card purchases and top-ups on the card screen.
- **Apple Pay / Google Pay** — add the card to your phone wallet and pay in stores and apps.

---

## If Something Went Wrong

**Issue fee paid, but no card.** Reopen **Cards** after a few minutes. If the card is still missing, create a [support ticket](../welcome/support.md) and attach the payment transaction.

**Top-up didn't arrive on the card.** Check both top-up transfers in History. If both are confirmed and the card balance hasn't changed, create a support ticket with the transaction hashes.

---

## Safety

- We never ask you to send funds to a chat or a support account to "activate" a card.
- Support will never ask for your card number, CVV or confirmation code.
- Card details appear only inside the official app.

No. Cards are available without Premium.

The card is available to verified users in supported countries. The app tells you if verification is not available for your country.
