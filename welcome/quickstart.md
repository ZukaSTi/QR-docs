# Quickstart

> From zero to your first payment in five steps.

---

## 1. Back Up Your Wallet

Write down your **seed phrase** on paper and keep it somewhere safe. It is the only way to restore your wallet on a new phone.

- Never screenshot it or store it in a chat or cloud notes
- Never share it — [QR] wallet support will never ask for it

More: [Key Management](../security/key-management.md)

## 2. Choose a Network and Copy Your Address

Open **Receive**, pick a network and copy your address (or show the QR code).

For stablecoins, Base and Solana have low fees. Make sure the sender uses **the same network** you picked. New users start in **Lite** mode with one Smart Account address — see [Smart Accounts](../core-concepts/smart-accounts.md).

## 3. Make a Small Test Deposit

Send a small amount first (for example $5–10) from an exchange or another wallet. A deposit of $5 or more also activates your Smart Account on most networks.

## 4. Check It in the Explorer

Open the transaction in **History** and tap the explorer link. **Success** means the funds are yours. Nothing arrived? See [My transfer didn't arrive](../features/send-and-receive.md#my-transfer-didnt-arrive).

## 5. Do Something With It

- Pay a merchant QR code — [QR Payments](../features/qr-payments.md)
- Issue a virtual card — [Virtual Cards](../features/cards.md)
- Send to a friend — [Send & Receive](../features/send-and-receive.md)
- Collect your first points — [Quests & Leagues](../premium-and-rewards/quests-and-leagues.md)

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Seed phrase** | The 12 words that restore your whole wallet (you can also import a 15–24-word phrase) |
| **Private key** | The secret that signs transactions. Derived from your seed phrase and stored only on your device |
| **EOA** | A regular address derived from your seed phrase |
| **Smart Account (SA)** | A contract wallet owned by your EOA. Supports sponsored (free) transactions |
| **Native token** | The token that pays gas on a network: ETH on Base, SOL on Solana, BNB on BNB Chain |
| **Wrapped token** | A token that represents another one, such as WETH for ETH. It can't pay gas |
| **Sponsored transaction** | A transaction where [QR] wallet pays the gas for you |
