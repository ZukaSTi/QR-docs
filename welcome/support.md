# Support

> How to get help — and how to get it fast.

---

## Create a Ticket

Open **Profile → Support → Create Ticket**. Describe the problem and attach photos or screenshots. We reply inside the app. If we don't reply within 48 hours, write to the email shown on the ticket screen.

**For a payment problem**, open the payment in **History** and tap **Payment issue**. The ticket is created with the network, transaction hash and amount already filled in. Each payment gets its own ticket.

You can have one open general ticket at a time — add new details to it instead of opening another.

---

## What to Include

- The **transaction hash** (in the transaction details)
- The **network** and **token**
- The **amount** and approximate **time**
- A screenshot of the error, if there was one

---

## Ticket Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Open | Waiting for support |
| In Progress | Support is working on it |
| Resolved | Support has answered — reply if the issue remains |
| Closed | Create a new ticket if the issue comes back |

---

## Stay Safe

{% hint style="danger" %}
[QR] wallet support will **never** ask for your secret phrase, private key, card number, CVV or confirmation code, and will never ask you to send funds. Anyone who does is a scammer — even if they write to you in our community chat.
{% endhint %}
