# Ambassador Program

> We invite ambassadors — and we tailor terms for serious partners.

---

## Who It's For

The Ambassador Program is **invite-only**. We look for people and teams who already bring users to [QR] wallet: communities, creators, regional leads, and companies.

If that sounds like you, write to **partners@qr.xyz** or use the in-app partner form. Tell us your region, audience size, and how you plan to grow [QR] wallet.

---

## How It Works

Ambassadors earn a **share of value** created by users they bring:

- **Premium and NFT purchases** — a percentage of the payment
- **QR payment volume** — a smaller percentage of processed volume

A referred user becomes **qualified** after they complete their first QR payment. Your tier follows how many qualified referrals you have.

Rewards accrue in the ambassador cabinet in the app. Withdrawals are reviewed (not instant on-chain).

This is **separate** from the standard [Referral Program](../premium-and-rewards/referral-program.md). You do not stack both on the same purchase.

---

## Tiers

Default public grid (qualified referrals):

| Tier | Qualified referrals | Premium / NFT | QR payments |
|------|:-------------------:|:-------------:|:-----------:|
| **Gold** | 1 – 100 | 10% | 0.1% |
| **Sapphire** | 101 – 1,000 | 20% | 0.2% |
| **Emerald** | 1,001 – 10,000 | 30% | 0.3% |
| **Ruby** | 10,001 – 100,000 | 40% | 0.4% |
| **Diamond** | 100,001+ | 50% | 0.5% |

Holding ambassador status places you on the entry tier even before the first qualified referral.

---

## Custom Terms

For **large partners** we can agree **custom percentages**, regions, and payout terms that sit outside the grid above. This is negotiated individually — not a self-serve toggle.

If you already run a sizable community or distribution channel, mention volume and geography in the first email so we can route you to a custom offer.

---

## What Ambassadors Get in the App

- Cabinet with earnings, tier progress, and referral stats
- Ability to reach their referral base (broadcast), when enabled
- Support from the partnership team
