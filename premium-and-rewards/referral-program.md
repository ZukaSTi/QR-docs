# Referral Program

> Invite friends — earn together.

---

## How It Works

Every [QR] wallet user gets a **unique referral link**. Share it — and earn rewards for every friend who joins.

---

## Two Referral Levels

| Level | Who | What You Get |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| **Level 1** | Your direct referrals | A share of their purchases and payments |
| **Level 2** | Your referrals' referrals | A smaller share |

This means: if you invited Alice, and Alice invited Bob — you earn cashback from both Alice (Level 1) and Bob (Level 2).

---

## Referral Code

You have **two types** of referral codes:

1. **Standard code** — auto-generated at registration
2. **Username code** — if you set a username, it becomes your referral code

Both codes can be shared as links.

### Link, Referral Code, Promo Code

- **Referral link / referral code** — links a new user to you as their referrer.
- **Username code** — with Premium, your username works as your referral code.
- **Promo code** — gives a bonus or discount. It does not make anyone your referral.

### When the Link Is Set

- Automatically, when your friend installs the app from your link or signs up with your code.
- Manually: your friend can enter your code in the Referral section during the **first 14 days** after creating the wallet.
- A referrer can't be changed once it is set.

### When a Referral Counts

A referral becomes **active** after your friend deposits **$5 or more** to their wallet. Until then, the referral list shows their progress:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Waiting for deposit | Signed up, no deposit yet |
| Deposit under $5 | Deposited, but less than $5 |
| Deposit received, activating | $5 deposit counted, activation in progress |
| Under review | Activation is being checked |
| Active | You earn rewards from this referral |

## Cashback

| Your friend… | Level 1 | Level 2 | Calculated from |
|---|:-:|:-:|---|
| Buys Premium or an NFT | 10% | 5% | Purchase price |
| Issues a virtual card | 10% | 5% | Card issue fee |
| Tops up a card | 0.1% | 0.05% | Top-up amount |
| Pays with QR | 0.1% | 0.05% | Payment amount |

Swaps and bridges don't earn referral rewards. Card top-up and QR rewards start once the referral is **active**.

**Examples:**
- Your friend buys Premium Black Year ($145) → you get **$14.50**. Their friend buys it → you get **$7.25**.
- Your friend makes a $100 QR payment → you get **$0.10**.

Rewards are paid in USD value. Exact percentages can vary with promotions. Ambassadors use a different grid — see [Ambassador Program](../for-partners/ambassador-program.md).

## Cashback Withdrawal

- **Minimum withdrawal:** $10
- Paid in **USDC on Base** to your **default address**
- A reward becomes available for withdrawal **24 hours** after it is earned
- One request at a time
- Each request is reviewed by our team

1. Go to the Referral section
2. Tap **Request Withdrawal** and enter the amount
3. Wait for review

Withdrawal statuses:
- **Pending** — request submitted
- **Approved** — accepted, payout is being sent
- **Completed** — funds sent
- **Rejected / Expired** — not paid; the amount stays on your balance

## Ambassador Program

We **invite ambassadors** — creators, communities, and regional partners.

Default tiers (Gold → Diamond) share Premium/NFT purchases and QR volume. **Large partners** can get **custom terms**.

This is invite-only and **does not stack** with standard referral cashback on the same purchase.

Full grid and how to apply: [Ambassador Program](../for-partners/ambassador-program.md).

## Points for Referrals

- "Invite Friend" quest — 500 points for every friend who deposits $5+
- "Share on Social" quest — 50 points for sharing your referral link

## FAQ

**When is cashback credited?**

After your referral buys Premium or an NFT, issues or tops up a card, or makes a QR payment. It becomes available for withdrawal 24 hours later.

**Is there a referral limit?**

No. Invite as many as you want.

**Is this the same as cashback?**

No. [Cashback](../features/cashback.md) is paid to **you** for your own QR payments. Referral rewards are paid for your referrals' activity. The [Ambassador Program](../for-partners/ambassador-program.md) replaces referral rewards for approved partners — they don't stack.

**Can I see who referred me?**

Yes, referrer information is displayed in the referral section.
