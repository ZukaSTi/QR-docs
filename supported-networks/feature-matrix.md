## Notes

- **Pay with QR** means you can pay a merchant QR code from your balance on this network. The payment screen shows exactly which tokens are accepted.
- **Staking** — AAVE on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon and BNB Chain; Compound on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism and Polygon; Marinade on Solana. Staking is shown in Pro mode.
- **Sponsored gas** uses your daily quota — see [Sponsored Transactions](../core-concepts/sponsored-transactions.md).
